Caveats

  • The 4 h split across tasks, residual human effort, review minutes, volume (100/month) and $55/h loaded cost are assumptions to be replaced by the pilot time study.
  • Intake, cross-document validation, exceptions and output files are not built yet; their savings are projections.
  • Test documents are 6 synthetic layouts designed to stress digit reading; re-run on the held-out real wire set before sign-off.
  • Human review, approval, bank import and release remain mandatory in every scenario.
